# Prototype

What the prototype must show and how it is judged. This is project content; CLAUDE.md only points here. Fill it in for your project.

## Purpose

- Who the prototype is for, and what decision it supports (for example: deciding when to start building the real product).

## What success looks like

- It shows the most important elements of the solutions to the problems, and how they connect.
- The workflow is clear and simple. Simplicity is the most important quality.

## Build order

The build order follows the push order (see versions/log.md). Nothing is built until it exists as a feature with an ID in solutions.md.

## Related rules

- What to mock or leave out (for example outside integrations or file storage).
- Who can see or change what.
