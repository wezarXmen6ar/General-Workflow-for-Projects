# Review

Fill this in after building a version, before saving it (`save-prototype`) or releasing it (`record-release`). Both refuse until the date and who reviewed are filled in. The saved copy is frozen with that version, and this file starts again empty.

Findings go to the backlog: ideas and changes to its Inbox, defects to its Bugs section.

- Version: (the version reviewed, for example v0.2)
- Date: (YYYY-MM-DD)
- Reviewed by: (names)
- Accepted: (feature IDs that meet their "Done when")
- Not accepted: (feature IDs, and why)
- Findings: (what was noticed; each finding is also added to the backlog)
