# Product design

How the real product is built. plan/ says what and why; this says how. Written at v1.0, before building, and changed only with the user's approval.

## Stack

- Languages, frameworks, and main libraries, and why.

## Architecture

- The main parts and how they talk to each other.

## Data

- What is stored, where, and who can see it.

## Integrations

- Outside systems the product connects to.

## Environments and releases

- Where it runs (for example test and production), and how a release reaches users.

## Tests

- How features are tested. Every feature gets at least one test named with its ID, based on its "Done when".
